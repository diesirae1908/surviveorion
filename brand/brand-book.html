00

What ORION is

The product, in short

A daily dodging game. Drone swarms close in, you have no gun, and the only thing that keeps you alive is moving well. Everyone gets the same run on the same day: same seed, same mutator, same conditions, three attempts per UTC day.

The ship has inertia, and that is what gives the movement its feel, but it is not the pitch. Nobody arrives for a physics model. They arrive to dodge, and they come back because today's run is the same one everybody else is failing at.

Before this document ORION had a look but not a brand. The colours lived in src/config.ts, the wordmark existed only inside a PNG, and nothing said which of the twelve golds was the gold. Everything here is derived from the shipped game, so adopting it changes almost nothing on screen and settles every open question about what to do next.

Skill is the only cheat code.

Product principle 1, and the whole brand in five words

The positioning claim

The daily dodging game. Plenty of games make you dodge. None of them give every player the same run on the same day and put the results on one board.

The one-line version

Wordle's daily ritual, applied to dodging. Use this when you have one sentence. Use the positioning claim when you have a paragraph.

01

The three pillars

How to decide anything

Three things. If a design or a line of copy does not sit inside one of them, it does not ship.

Pillar one

No gun. You dodge.

Survival is movement, not firepower. Powers are defensive.

So: show near-misses, not massacres. Never power-fantasy imagery or "unleash your power" language. The hero screenshot is a ship threading a gap, not a screen full of explosions.

Pillar two

Everyone gets the same day.

One seed, one mutator, three attempts, worldwide. That is the whole social hook.

So: the brand is communal, not solitary. Copy says every pilot, today's patrol, the board. Mutators get names, not difficulty numbers, because everyone is talking about the same one today.

Pillar three

Say less, and say it warm.

Short lines, present tense, no hype. But warm on purpose.

So: warm whites, lit golds, a dark that is deep blue rather than black. A cockpit at night, not a hospital corridor. Terse is not the same as cold.

02

Naming

How things get named

Mutators

All-caps codename plus a plain-language subline. The codename carries the identity, the subline says exactly what changes. Never explain the joke in the codename. Mutator names are never funny.

  • CRYO WINTER
    Every pickup is a Cryo Field.
  • THE PIT
    The arena is about 30% smaller in both dimensions.

Badges

Short human phrases, often wry, describing a real feat. Badge names may be funny, and the funniest one in the game is for dying in ten seconds.

  • Space Dust
    Perish within 10 seconds. It happens to the best of us.
  • Galaxy's Finest
    Hold the #1 spot on the leaderboard.
ORIONCaps in the wordmark and in product UI. "Orion" is fine in running prose, "ORION" is preferred in headlines.
Daily PatrolThe name of the primary mode. Never "daily challenge".
PowersTwo-word Title Case: one noun of function, one noun of thing. Aegis Shield, Missile Swarm, Arc Lightning, Cryo Field.
PilotWhat players are called. Never users, never gamers.
CallsignWhat their name is called. Never nickname, never username.

Primary tagline

Survive the swarm.

Descriptor

A daily survival patrol.

Social bio

Three attempts a day. Same run for every pilot.

03

Identity

Mark, wordmark, lockups

The mark

The Patrol Sight

A gold ring cut on the four diagonals, red core dead centre. It reads three ways at once, which is why it works: a targeting reticle, an eclipse, and the top-down silhouette of a ship inside its arena.

It evolves the favicon already shipped (a plain gold ring and a red dot) rather than replacing it, so nothing currently live looks wrong beside it. The diagonal cuts are the same 45-degree corner cut used in the wordmark. That cut is the brand's one repeating geometric idea: the chamfer. Use it on cards, buttons, medals and crops. Never a rounded pill.

The wordmark

It is outlines, not a font

Five chamfered geometric letterforms on a 100-unit cap height, 22-unit stems, 22-unit corner cuts. It ships as vector outlines, so it carries no font dependency at all. Do not re-set it in Rajdhani or anything else.

Lockups and finishes

Which finish on which background

Horizontal by default. Gradient on dark, -mono-black on light, -mono-white on photography or anything busy. Switch the ground below and the lockup follows its own rule.

Clearspace and minimum size

How much space to leave around it

X is the diameter of the red core. Keep X clear on all four sides of every lockup. Nothing enters that box, including background artwork with detail in it.

XX

Horizontal lockup: minimum 120 px wide, or 30 mm in print. Shown at size.

Mark: minimum 24 px. Shown at size. Below this the diagonal cuts close up, so switch to the solid-ring favicon.

Misuse

What not to do

Do not recolour outside the four supplied finishes.

Do not stretch or condense either element.

Do not rotate the wordmark.

No shadows, bevels or glow beyond the single gold bloom on the icon art.

Do not put the gradient lockup on light. -mono-black exists for that.

ORION

Do not rebuild the wordmark in a font. It is outlines for a reason.

04

Colour

Click any swatch to copy

Gold is the protagonist, red is the antagonist, and the split is roughly 70 / 25 / 5 gold to red to everything else. Red should feel like it is arriving from off-frame. In every layout here it enters from the right, because that is the direction the swarm comes from in the boot cinematic.

Core

Ratios are measured against Void #0a0a12, WCAG 2.1.

Hard rule

Never set body copy in Rising Red on Void. At 3.37:1 it fails AA for anything below large text. It is the colour of the core and the swarm, not of sentences. Red text uses Alarm #ff4455 at 5.83:1, which is why live mutator names on the share card are Alarm.

Watch this one

Dust clears AA on Void by 0.19. On Deep Space it drops to 4.42 and fails. Keep Dust on Void only; on raised surfaces use Bronze. Everything on this page follows that, which is why panel captions are Bronze and page captions are Dust.

Power signals

Functional, never decorative. A colour means a power. Using Magnet purple as a background accent teaches pilots the wrong thing.

Medal metals

Awarded on score, per mutator factor. The plate is a hexagon because the drones are hexagons: you are wearing what you survived.

06

Voice

Three of them

ORION speaks in three voices. They share a spine and differ in warmth. Every line ORION ships is in one of them. If you cannot tell which one you are writing in, you are writing in none of them.

Rules for all three voices

  1. The player is a pilot. Never a user, a player, or a gamer. Their name is a callsign.
  2. Short lines. If a sentence needs a comma to survive, try two sentences first.
  3. Present tense, active voice. "Three attempts a day", not "You will be given three attempts per day."
  4. Specific over superlative. "Destroy 1,000 drones in a single run" beats "an epic challenge". Numbers are more exciting than adjectives.
  5. No em dashes, ever. Use a comma, a colon, parentheses, a middle dot, or a full stop. Hard rule, already swept out of the game once on Aug 17, 2026.
  6. One exclamation mark per surface, maximum. Usually zero.
  7. Never fake scale. No testimonials, no press quotes, no player counts unless real and current. ORION is small and honest about it.
  8. Absolute dates with a timezone. "Aug 18, 3:44 PM PT", never "yesterday". Daily Patrol rolls at 00:00 UTC, which is 5 PM PT.

Voice 01

Mission Control

Where
In-game UI, HUD, prompts, errors, briefings, push.
Who
The ground station. It has your telemetry on a screen. Calm, busy, on your side, not going to make a fuss.
Register
Flight comms. Terse, second person, present tense, world already in motion.

Rules

  • Fact first, consequence second.
  • Contractions preferred.
  • All-caps for labels only, never a sentence.
  • Failure states name the cause, then the fallback. Never blame the pilot for an infrastructure problem.
  • Never explain a mechanic the pilot can feel.

Sounds like

  • Today's patrol, still flying.LIVE
  • Score not saved. Couldn't reach the leaderboard.LIVE
  • Sandboxed: no daily attempt spent, no score submitted, no medal recorded.LIVE
  • No motion data from this device. Flying with the touch stick.LIVE
  • Out of attempts. Next patrol at 00:00 UTC.

Never

  • Uh oh! Something went wrong. Please try again later.
  • Get ready for the ULTIMATE arcade challenge!

Voice 02

The Log

Where
The site, store listings, link previews, patch notes, Reddit posts, ads, the README.
Who
The person who built it, writing down what happened.
Register
A builder's changelog that happens to be readable. It reports, it does not pitch.

Rules

  • Open with the concrete thing, not the promise.
  • Credit influences openly. The tilt controls already say "a tribute to Tilt to Live". Keep that.
  • Never claim a feature that is not live.
  • A launch post says what it is, what is different, what it costs, where to play. Four things, in that order.
  • Never "we're thrilled", "we're excited to announce".

Sounds like

  • ORION is live at surviveorion.com. It is a daily survival arcade: one shared seed a day, three attempts, no gun. You dodge.
  • 22 named mutators, one per day, two on Sundays. Sidegrades, not difficulty sliders.
  • Tilt steering: lean the phone to fly. A tribute to Tilt to Live.LIVE

Never

  • ORION redefines the arcade genre for a new generation.
  • Experience heart-pounding action like never before.

Voice 03

Wingmate

Where
Feedback replies, support email, Reddit comments, Discord, anything answering a human.
Who
The pilot in the next seat. Treats the person as a competent adult who found something real.
Register
Mission Control with the shoulders down. It can be funny.

Rules

  • Thank them for the specific thing, not for "reaching out".
  • Say what you will do, or say you will not and why. Never "we'll look into it".
  • Never apologise twice for the same thing.
  • Match their register. A one-line gripe gets a one-line answer.
  • Never argue with a bad review in public.

Sounds like

  • Received, pilot. Best ideas make it into the arena.LIVE
  • Thank you, pilot. Your report is in the log.LIVE
  • Good catch. The touch-drag lockup is real, it is fixed on the branch, it goes live this week.
  • Not planned right now, and I would rather say so than leave it on a roadmap forever.

Never

  • Thank you for reaching out! We appreciate your feedback and will pass it along to our team.
  • We're sorry for any inconvenience this may have caused.

Which voice

Mission ControlA button, a toast, a HUD label, an error, a mutator briefing, a badge description.
The LogThe site, an ad, a store page, a link preview, a patch note, a Reddit post you wrote.
WingmateAny reply to a pilot, anywhere. Including a Reddit comment on your own post.

Use

pilot · callsign · patrol · run · attempt · the swarm · the board · the arena · mutator · power · medal · drone · seed · graze · multiplier

Never ships

user · gamer · level · enemy · epic · insane · game-changing · content · experience · journey · "we're thrilled" · "please try again later" · any em dash

French

Same voice, same brevity, same ban. pilote, indicatif, la patrouille du jour. Never translate a mutator codename: they are proper nouns, the same way STARFALL stays STARFALL on the board everyone shares.
