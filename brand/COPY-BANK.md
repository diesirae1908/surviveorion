## Elevator descriptions

**One sentence**

> the same run for every pilot on earth.

**Meta description (live)**
> ORION: a daily survival patrol. Dodge the drone swarm, grab powers, chase the daily
> leaderboard. Three attempts a day, same run for every pilot. Free, no install, plays great on
> your phone.

**Paragraph**
> No gun. Just a ship, a bounded arena, and drone swarms that keep coming. You stay alive by
> moving well and by grabbing defensive powers, nothing else. Every UTC day gives every pilot
> the same seed and the same named mutator, so a score is a real comparison and not a shopping
> list. Three attempts. Free in the browser, installable on a phone.

## App store / itch style

**Short description**
> A daily dodging game. Same run for every pilot, three attempts a day, one shared
> leaderboard.

**Feature bullets**
> - One shared seed per day. Everyone flies the same conditions.
> - 22 named mutators, one per day, two on Sundays. Sidegrades, not difficulty sliders.
> - Copper, Silver and Gold medals on score.
> - Tilt or touch on mobile, WASD on desktop.
> - Free, no install, no account required to fly.

**How it works**
> Every UTC day gets one seed and one named mutator. Every pilot flies the same conditions.
> You get three attempts. Your best one goes on the board.

**Why there is no gun**
> Because rank should be earned by flying. Powers are defensive: shields, cryo fields, vortexes,
> the occasional missile swarm you did not ask for. Skill is the only cheat code.
