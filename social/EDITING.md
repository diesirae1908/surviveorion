# orion-social Phase B v2: the editing grammar

*Written 2026-08-25 (Aug 24 PT), after reviewing the first goldens. This replaces the
"Phase B: rendering" section of SPEC.md. Everything else in SPEC.md stands.*

The v1 goldens were correct to spec and wrong for the medium: a letterboxed landscape strip in
a 9:16 frame, one static caption for the whole video, 22 seconds with nothing changing. What
works in short-form in 2026, per current practice: the hook lands in the first half-second,
something on screen changes at least every 2.5 seconds, impacts get slow-motion at 40 to 60
percent, and the last frame loops into the first. Raw gameplay with meme energy beats polish.

## The five laws (every video, every format)

1. **Full-bleed or nothing.** The 9:16 frame is 100 percent gameplay. Crop a moving window out
   of the source; never letterbox, never pad. Small gameplay in a big empty frame is death.
2. **Cold open on the peak.** The first 0.5 to 1 second is the most intense moment of the clip
   (the graze, the death, the chaos), punched in, with the beat-1 hook text already on screen.
   Then a 2-frame flash "rewind" and the story starts. Never open on context.
3. **Visual velocity.** A text beat, zoom step, speed change, cut, or overlay pop must happen
   at least every 2.5 seconds. If a stretch has none, cut the stretch.
4. **Ramp the moment.** The payoff moment plays at 45 to 50 percent speed with a punch zoom
   (1.3 to 1.6x) and a 4 to 8 px shake on the nearest pass. Setup plays at 110 to 130 percent.
   Nothing plays at flat 100 percent for more than ~4 seconds.
5. **End into the loop.** Freeze the final frame 0.3 to 0.4s with the CTA text, and make it
   visually rhyme with the cold open so the replay is seamless. Never fade out.

## Duration targets (hard caps)

SPACE DUST 6-9s · CLOSE CALL 8-11s · THE BOARD 9-12s · TODAY'S PATROL 10-14s. The v1 22s
patrol cut is retired. Under 12s is the loop-rate sweet spot; if a beat sheet does not fit,
cut gameplay, not beats.

## The crop engine (the big change)

Render a 9:16 window that follows the action:

- **v2.0 (now, no game change):** for CLOSE CALL cuts, anchor the window on the graze's
  `x,y` from the sidecar (it is already there), converted from world to pixel space, with a
  slow 6 percent-per-second push-in. For other cuts, anchor arena-center with the same push-in.
  Clamp the window inside the frame; ease anchor changes over 400ms (no snaps).
- **v2.1 (after the game-side track dispatch):** the sidecar gains `track: [[t,x,y], ...]` at
  2 Hz plus `arena:{w,h}` and `view:{w,h}`. The window follows the smoothed track (critically
  damped spring, max pan speed capped so it glides rather than chases). This makes every cut
  follow the ship.
- Implementation: precompute the window path per output frame in plan code (pure, testable),
  emit it as an ffmpeg `sendcmd` script driving `crop` x/y (or `zoompan` for push-ins).
  Punch zooms and shake are offsets added onto the same path.

## The caption engine

Replace single drawtext with **ASS subtitles burned via libass** (`subtitles=` filter). One
`.ass` per video, generated from the beat sheet:

- Rajdhani Bold, 84 to 110 px at 1080 wide, uppercase, Starlight `#fff7e0`, 4 px Void outline,
  no box. Mutator names and death beats in Alarm `#ff4455`. Numbers in Hull Gold `#ffd700`.
- Every line pops in: `\fscx80\fscy80` to 100 over ~120 ms, and fades out 100 ms before its
  beat ends. Max 2 lines, max 4 words per line, one idea per beat.
- Position: upper third (y around 16 to 26 percent), inside all platform safe zones. Never
  over the ship's likely position (the crop engine knows where the ship is; keep text opposite).
- Beat text comes from `HOOKS.md` templates filled with sidecar numbers. Specific numbers beat
  adjectives: "0.04% FROM DEATH" outperforms "SO CLOSE".

## Meme overlays

`assets/memes/` (transparent PNGs, generated by `make-meme-overlays.cjs`, committed): impact-
style text cards (AT THIS MOMENT / HE KNEW / WAIT FOR IT / BRO THOUGHT HE HAD IT / SKILL
ISSUE / RIP / CLEAN / COULD YOU DODGE THIS?), a hand-wobble red circle, a red arrow, a REPLAY
tag, a soft edge-vignette. Rules: pop-in with an SFX hit, hold 0.6 to 1.2s, max 2 per video
(SPACE DUST may use 3). The red circle goes on the killer drone 0.3s before impact; the
AT THIS MOMENT / HE KNEW pair is SPACE DUST's signature move, timed 1.2s and 0.4s before death
with a hard pause frame (0.5s freeze) between them.

## Sound design

- Game audio stays, ducked 6 dB under SFX hits.
- CC0 SFX bank (`assets/sfx/`, each file with a source note): riser (cold open), boom/impact
  (punch zoom, meme card pops), rewind/tape-stop (the flash cut), whoosh (speed ramp in/out),
  a descending "wah" (SPACE DUST deaths). No third-party meme audio baked, ever; trending
  sounds are attached in-app per SOCIAL.md.
- THE BOARD gets a Suno track bed at 0.35 under the whole cut.

## Beat sheets (the presets, replacing v1's cut list)

**CLOSE CALL** (8-11s)
1. 0.0-0.9 cold open: the graze at 1.5x punch-in, 100% speed, beat-1 hook ("{pct}% FROM
   DEATH"), riser.
2. 0.9-1.1 white 2-frame flash + rewind SFX.
3. 1.1-5.0 context from t-6 at 1.25x, follow-crop, beat-2 text at 2.5 ("watch the top").
4. 5.0-7.0 the graze: ramp to 45% through [t-0.4, t+0.6], punch 1.4x, red circle on the drone
   at t-0.3, shake at closest pass, boom.
5. 7.0-9.0 aftermath at 100%, beat-3 ("he lived." or, on a death ending, "he did not." in
   Alarm).
6. freeze 0.4s: "could you? ▶". Loop-rhyme with the cold open.

**SPACE DUST** (6-9s)
1. 0.0-0.8 cold open: the death at 1.5x speed, hook "day {N}: {s} SECONDS".
2. 0.8-1.0 flash rewind.

4. 4.5-5.0 hard freeze: AT THIS MOMENT card, boom.
5. 5.0-6.5 resume 50% into the death: HE KNEW card at impact, wah SFX, RIP stamp on the
   wreck.
6. freeze: "{s}s. the daily patrol is undefeated. ▶".

**THE BOARD** (9-12s)

   steps.
4. 8.0-9.5 death or survive-out; slam to a full-frame score card (share-card styling): score
   huge in gold, "day {N} · {MUTATOR}" below, boom.

**TODAY'S PATROL** (10-14s)

2. 1.4-1.6 flash.

   the subline compressed to max 4 words (ARSENAL: "double the pickups."), then "everyone

4. 8.5-11 CTA card over calming gameplay: "3 attempts. free. today only." + surviveorion.com.
