# Hook bank

*Beat-1 lines land in the first half-second, already on screen at frame one. Rules: numbers
beat adjectives, 5 words max for beat 1, one idea per beat, mutator names always all-caps in
Alarm. No em dashes, one "!" per video max (almost always zero). `{vars}` fill from the
sidecar. The CTA beat is comment bait or a loop prompt, never "follow for more".*

## CLOSE CALL

Beat 1 (pick by data):
- `{pct}% FROM DEATH` (clearance x 100, one decimal; the money hook, use when pct <= 8)
- `ONE PIXEL.`
- `THIS DODGE IS ILLEGAL`
- `HOW.`
- `DEATH MISSED BY {pct}%`

Beat 2 (context): `watch the top` / `count the drones` / `he doesn't see it yet`
Beat 3 (aftermath): `he lived.` / `he did not.` (Alarm) / `and then he kept going`
CTA: `could you? ▶` / `rate the dodge /10` / `what would you have done`
