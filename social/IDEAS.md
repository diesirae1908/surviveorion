# 30 post ideas: a month of ORION content (v2, research-checked)

*2026-08-25, revised same day after Lucas pushed back ("did you actually research?"). v1 was
pattern knowledge. This version is rebuilt against 8 searches: indie TikTok case studies
(Bopl Battle 500k copies zero paid, The Matriarch 400 to 20k wishlists off one video,
Schrodinger's Cat Burglar 2k wishlists overnight), Wordle's virality mechanics, 2026
gaming-shorts craft data, and platform algorithm rules. What changed: a new group built on
ORION's actual structural advantage (the Wordle-style "everyone flies the same run" loop,
badly under-used in v1), bug content added, explicit comment-bait phrasing removed
(penalized in 2026), reactive trend slots added, and craft rules from retention data.*

## What the evidence says (and how it maps to ORION)

1. **The first 1 to 2 seconds decide everything.** Best shorts hold 70 to 90% of viewers in
   second one. Start ON the action, cut the wind-up. 80% held for 3s beats 60% held for 30s.
2. **85% of short-form is watched muted.** The tagline is not decoration, it is the video
   for most viewers. One line, top of frame, readable on mute, always.
3. **15 to 34 seconds is the virality band** (70 to 85% completion). Our approved seeds
   (14 to 19s) sit in it. Keep it.
4. **Wordle's engine was the shared daily artifact + "we all played the same puzzle."**
   Spoiler-safe share format, scarcity (can't binge), friends' posts as daily reminders.
   ORION has this EXACT structure (one seed, 3 attempts, everyone) and v1 barely used it.
   This is our biggest differentiator vs generic gameplay clips.
5. **Authenticity beats polish; bugs are content.** Bopl Battle: dev bug compilations and
   matter-of-fact mechanic clips outperform produced promos. "Who needs new features when
   you got old bugs?"
6. **Genuine comment conversations are rewarded; explicit engagement bait is penalized
   (stricter in 2025-26).** Curiosity gaps and native formats ("wrong answers only") are
   fine; "comment below to..." commands are not. Replying fast to early comments compounds
   reach.
7. **Consistency beats virality; organic reach for games has compressed (~4-8%).** A steady
   post every day beats a masterpiece a month. Expect a grind, count on the compounding.
   Every post carries the CTA (free browser game, link in bio) because the winners always
   ask.
8. **Clutch highlights, relatable fails, and educational clips are the three validated
   gaming-shorts categories.** Educational gets searched and saved (staying power); fails
   and clutches spike. Our WASTED / NEW BEST machinery is exactly the fail/clutch pair.

## A. The engines (repeatable fail/clutch, ~12 slots) — validated by #1, #3, #8

1. **WASTED classic** [W] REPEATABLE. Any dumb death, video opens mid-chaos not at run
   start. Rotate taglines: "flying too close to the stars", "he had a plan. the plan had
   him.", "the swarm always collects".
2. **NEW BEST** [NB] REPEATABLE. Every time a run beats the HUD BEST. Celebration sound
   attached in-app.
3. **0.0X% FROM DEATH** [F] REPEATABLE. Closest-call freeze, red circle on the gap. Needs
   the full sidecar (Chrome download fix) for graze timestamps.
4. **Space Dust speedrun** [W variant] REPEATABLE. Die under 10 seconds, show the real
   badge popup: "the game gave me a badge for this." Relatable-fail category, and the badge
   is the punchline.
5. **Rate this dodge** [F] REPEATABLE. Native format, no command phrasing needed: tagline
   is just "rate this dodge." The comments do the rest.
6. **Three attempts, one day** [W x3]. All three daily attempts back to back, 4s each,
   scores stacking. Ends on glory or triple disaster. Scarcity is visible: there is no
   attempt four.
7. **One attempt left** [W/F]. "two deaths. one attempt left today." Slow push, silence,
   then either ending works. Urgency hook straight from the retention playbooks.

## F. Floating slots: reactive trends (2-3 per month, unplanned on purpose) — per #5

Bopl Battle's growth was partly reactive: when a sound, meme, or format spikes in the
gaming corner of TikTok, ORION footage gets cut to it within 48h. These REPLACE scheduled
slots that week (the queue flexes, total stays 1/day). Cannot be pre-listed by definition;
the standing order is: when Lucas sees a trend that fits, send the sound link, footage
library does the rest.

## Operating rules (not posts, but the evidence was loudest here)

- **First frame = action.** Whatever the planned start is, start later. No logo intros, no
  run start, no menu.
- **Tagline always, readable on mute.** 85% watch silent.
- **15 to 34 seconds.** Our grammar already lives there; resist longer cuts.
- **CTA every post**: "free in your browser, link in bio, today's run is live." Same-day
  posts for anything patrol-specific so the CTA is playable.
- **Reply to comments fast, especially in hour one.** Early conversation compounds reach.
  Answers in ORION voice (The Log). This is Lucas's 10 min/day, no tooling can fake it.
- **Consistency over banger-hunting.** The month plan exists so a mediocre-but-on-brand
  post ships daily. Virality is the bonus, not the plan.
- **Expect the grind**: organic game reach in 2026 is compressed. Wishlist-style compounding
  (followers, saved educational posts, duet chains) is the actual win condition.

## Production math

One 30-40 min session (4-6 runs) feeds group A for a week; groups B-E cut from the same
footage. New assets for the month: countdown overlay, 3-clip stack template, duet caption
template, power/badge name cards, two text-card templates, one 5s phone B-roll. Patrol-day
items post same-day; the rest is scheduleable, so the queue genuinely holds 30 days with
2-3 slots kept loose for trends.
