ORION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

ORION is a free browser game at surviveorion.com, run by its developer, Lucas Navilloz. This page says exactly what the game collects, what it never collects, and how to reach us. It is written to be read, not scrolled past.

The short version

You can play without an account. If you make one, we store your callsign and your scores. We run no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data with anyone.

What we collect, and why

  • Callsign and account data. If you create an account: your chosen callsign, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), and an optional two-letter country code you pick for the leaderboard flag. If you sign in with Google, we store the account identifier Google gives us instead of a password. Guest accounts are tied to a secret stored only in your browser; we keep a hash of it.
  • Gameplay results. Scores, survival times, kills, multipliers, badges, daily-patrol results, arena and wingmate links. This is what leaderboards are made of.
  • Feedback you send. The message, and your email only if you choose to leave one for a reply. Crash reports sent through the feedback channel include technical context about the error.
  • Basic visit counts. Our own server counts visits with a truncated hash of your IP address, the page path, referrer, platform, and country. Raw IP addresses are never stored. There is no third-party analytics service.
  • Transient IP use. Your IP address is used in memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention while you play, then discarded.

What never leaves your device: run recordings. If you turn on clip recording, the video and its stats file are created in your browser and offered to you as downloads. They are never uploaded, and we never see them. Your settings, control bindings and local streaks also stay in your browser's storage.

Third parties

Two, and only when relevant to you:

  • Google Sign-In, only if you use it, under Google's privacy policy. We receive your Google account identifier and name; we do not receive your password.
  • Hosting. The game and its database run on Render, fronted by Cloudflare, which process traffic to serve the site. Google Fonts serves the typeface.

Retention and deletion

Account and score data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and its data, or to ask what we hold about you, use the in-game feedback form (Send feedback, on the game-over screen or the daily lobby) and leave an email so we can confirm. Deletion requests are honoured within 30 days.

Children

ORION is a general-audience arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; accounts need nothing beyond a made-up callsign.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes get a notice in the game.

Contact

The in-game feedback form is the fastest route and reaches the developer directly.
